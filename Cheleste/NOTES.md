# Cheleste: notes for changing the game

## Layout

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `Cheleste.ino` | setup/loop: buttons, the cart's `_update()`/`_draw()`, the pause menu, the save, the debug hook |
| `Game.h/.cpp` | the cart's Lua in C++: its objects, physics, rooms, title and summit |
| `Fix.h/.cpp` | `num`: PICO-8's 16.16 numbers; `p8sin`, `p8cos`, `rnd` |
| `P8.h/.cpp` | PICO-8's `spr map rectfill circfill line print pal camera mget fget` on `gfx_fb` |
| `Sounds.h/.cpp` | `sfx(n)` and `music(n)` on `audio::` |
| `Save.h/.cpp` | the saved game (magic `CHLS`) |
| `config.h` | every switch (`CHLS_*`) |
| `src/assets/Cart.*`, `Sine.cpp` | GENERATED by `../tools/p8extract.py` |
| `src/audio/Audio.*` | GENERATED by `../tools/p8audio.py` |

The cart (`../assets/celeste.p8`) is not in the repository. The generated files are, so a clone builds without it. To regenerate after changing a tool: `python tools/p8extract.py` and `python tools/p8audio.py`, from the project root.

## Faithful, and how that is checked

`Game.cpp` follows the cart's Lua line by line, including its quirks:
- `move_x` steps `0..abs(amount)` inclusive.
- `kill_player` calls `restart_room` eight times.
- A dead player's `update` runs to its end.
- `dash_effect_time` counts down forever and wraps as 16.16 does.

PICO-8's `foreach` is copied exactly from zepto8's `all()`. Deleting the current object skips nothing, and objects added during the loop are visited in the same frame. This is why a new player is updated on the frame its spawn ends. The C# Classic.cs does both of these differently.

1. **Golden traces**: `python tools/ref/compare.py` (needs `pip install lupa` and the cart).
   - It runs the cart's own Lua (Lua 5.4 with a PICO-8 shim) and `Game.cpp` built with `CHLS_NUM_DOUBLE=1` side by side, on the same button presses.
   - The presses cover the title and every room, half of the runs with the double dash.
   - Every object's position, speed, remainder and sprite is compared after every frame, along with the room, deaths, freeze, shake and every sound.
   - Measured: 128 runs of 900 frames agree (to 1e-9: the last bit of a `sin` is the C library's).
   - A change of 1e-7 to gravity is caught. `cl test` runs a 300-frame round.
2. **The 16.16 build** (`--fixed`): the board's numbers against the doubles.
   - The two part where PICO-8's own rounding shows: `0.05` is `0x0ccd`, so a remainder of -0.5 comes out -0.50002 and rounds the other way.
   - This is PICO-8's behaviour, which the board follows. The doubles of the Lua shim and of picolove, which the TAS tool used, don't.
3. **The TAS**: `python tools/ref/tas.py [--fixed] [--lua]`.
   - It plays the community's Any% TAS level by level.
   - On doubles, 29 of 30 levels clear, on the same frame as the cart's Lua. Level 11's route needs the TAS tool's seeded balloon heights.
   - On 16.16, 23 of 30 clear.
4. **The whole game on the board's numbers**: `python tools/ref/playthrough.py` chains the TAS from boot to summit through the port.
   - It uses the board's numbers and rnd seed.
   - Where a route misses (a balloon's bob phase comes from `rnd()`), the level's start is put off a few frames. The script's header names those levels.
   - It writes `tools/scripts/playthrough.txt`: 3395 frames, no deaths, about 1:53, with a picture of each level as it starts.
   - It also writes `tools/scripts/gameplay.txt` (the README's GIF).

## Measured (simulator, `cl run tools/scripts/rooms.txt`, after `cal`)

- **Render time:** estimated 13-19 ms on the board in every room, inside the 33 ms of a 30 fps frame. The odd single-frame max of 40-90 ms in a run was host jitter: it moves between runs.
- **Release build:** flash 45,448 B of 50,432 (both save pages kept), static RAM 17,360 of 18,416 B.
- **Debug build:** 48,260 B.
- **Where the flash goes:**
  - the cart's sheet and map: 12 KB;
  - the sound: about 9 KB (21 effects, 4 songs; songs 1.5 / 1.3 / 3.8 KB, wind and title about 0.1 KB);
  - the game: the rest.
- **Objects:** the pool holds 40; the most seen at once in the traces is 25 (room 3's fall floors and smoke).
- No soft-float in the image (`cl size`: libgcc has only `_divdi3`).

To measure on the board, use a debug build and `python Cheleste/tools/chsim/chdrive.py --device Cheleste/tools/scripts/rooms.txt out/rooms`, whose `perf` lines are then real.

## Sound

PICO-8 has 4 voices, 8 waveforms and a volume per note; the piezo has one square wave.
- **Effects** (`p8audio.py`): a step per note at PICO-8's note length (speed × 183/22050 s).
  - Slides become sweeps and drops become falls.
  - Noise becomes a scatter of 6 ms pitches between 300 and 3000 Hz.
  - Effects pitched below 180 Hz are moved up whole octaves.
  - All of them are `GLIDE`, which cut the orb's clicks from 733 to 17.
- **Songs:** each song's patterns from its loop start to its loop end, as Playtune scores played in LEAD mode.
  - The melody plays, and one more voice fills the melody's rests. The voice with the most notes, then the highest, is taken as the melody.
  - Noise (the drums) is left out, so the wind "song" is nearly silent, as it is mostly noise on PICO-8 too.
  - Fades are not kept.
- **Previews:** `cl audio out/audio` writes a WAV of each effect and song. The tuning still to do is by ear on the board: `USED`'s priorities, `SONG_VOICES`, the octave floor.

## Changes from the cart (all in `config.h`)

- The title says `a+b` instead of `x+c` (`CHLS_TITLE_KEYS`).
- `CHLS_SWAP_AB` puts dash on A and jump on B.
- `CHLS_EXTRAS` (on): START pauses (CONTINUE, RESTART ROOM, TITLE). Each new level is saved, and the title offers B to continue.
  - A continued game gets its level, double dash, deaths, time, strawberries, the song that would be playing and, after the orb, the pink sky.
  - The summit clears the save.
- `CHLS_MUSIC 0` keeps only the effects.
- A frozen frame (dash, orb) sends the last picture out again instead of nothing. It looks the same, and keeps the simulator's lockstep counting frames.

## Scripts (`cl run Cheleste/tools/scripts/<name>.txt out/<name>`)

| Script | What |
|---|---|
| `smoke` | the title, then 100 m |
| `rooms` | every level by the debug hook (`say R <level> [djump]`), a picture and a `perf` each |
| `playthrough` | the whole game from boot, a picture per level (generated; several minutes) |
| `gameplay` | the README GIF's four clips (generated; `cl gif`) |
| `pause` | the pause menu, TITLE, and the title's continue back to the saved level |

`cl check` runs them all (playthrough once), the host tests (`fix`, and the trace round when the cart and lupa are there) and the device build.
